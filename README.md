# My Liberation Notes (2022)
---

## Overview
**My Liberation Notes** is a beloved South Korean television series that beautifully explores the theme of self-discovery and the human desire for liberation from life's monotony.
 
## Basic Info
**Genre:** Drama, Slice of Life
**Episodes:** 16
**Network:** JTBC
**Release Year:** 2022
**Main Cast:** Lee Min-ki, Son Seok-koo, Kim Ji-won, Lee El
 
## Synopsis
The story revolves around three siblings who lead monotonous lives and struggle with their unfulfilled dreams and desires. Life changes when a mysterious stranger appears, prompting them to examine their lives and seek true freedom.
 
## Why I think it is a must watch?
- Deeply emotional storytelling.
- Realistic character development.
- Subtle social commentary.
- Beautiful cinematography and soundtrack.
 
## Favorite Quotes
> “True liberation is found in accepting yourself, flaws and all.”
